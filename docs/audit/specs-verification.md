# Market specs verification

Checked 2026-11-04 against `js/engines/specs.js`. Results: 75 verified, 11 corrected, 16 unverified. The machine-readable version is `specs-verified.json`.

How to read the status column:
- **verified**: the app value matches the source. Where only secondary sources were reachable, the note says "secondary".
- **corrected**: the app value is wrong or out of date. The verified value is the one to use.
- **unverified**: I could not confirm the value. The app value is kept as it is.

The `extra.*` rows are specs that are not in specs.js but that the audit asked me to check.

## Corrections at a glance

1. **NSE session times changed in Aug–Sep 2026.** A Closing Auction Session started on 3 Aug 2026: stocks with F&O contracts trade continuously to 15:15, and the auction runs 15:15–15:35. The post-close session is now **15:50–16:00** (was 15:40–16:00), and equity F&O closes at **15:40**. On 7 Sep 2026 pre-open order entry was extended to **09:10** (random close 09:08–09:10), with matching at 09:10–09:12.
2. **FINRA TAF is $0.00 per share and $0.00 cap for trades from 1 Oct to 31 Dec 2026** (SR-FINRA-2026-021). The app's $0.000195 / $9.79 is the standard rate, which returns on 1 Jan 2027. The SEC Section 31 rate of $20.60 per million (effective 4 Apr 2026) is correct.
3. **Index futures tick size is not a flat 0.05.** It depends on the index level: 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.20 (NSE/FAOP/67135, 15 Apr 2025). Index options stay at 0.05.
4. **SGX has no lunch break.** It trades 09:00–17:00 continuously, so the app's split session is wrong.
5. **ASX close.** The closing single price auction runs 16:10 to 16:11, so the post window should end at 16:11, not 16:12.

India STT, NSE transaction charges with IPFT, stamp duty, SEBI fee, GST and the four NSE lot sizes all match. The US PDT note is correct, but should add the 4 Jun 2026 effective date and the 20 Oct 2027 phase-in deadline.

### Special sessions and early closes (not included in the holiday arrays)

- **NSE Muhurat trading, Sunday 8 Nov 2026** (NSE circular 149/2026, NSE/CMTR/76941, 19 Oct 2026):
  - Block deal 13:50–14:05.
  - Pre-open 14:05–14:20 (order entry 14:05–14:15, matching 14:15–14:17).
  - Normal market from 14:20. It closes at 15:15 for CAS stocks and 15:30 for other stocks.
  - Closing auction 15:20–15:35. Post-close 15:50–16:00.
  - All trades settle normally.
- **NSE 2026-11-10 (Diwali Balipratipada) and 2026-11-24 (Guru Nanak Jayanti):** the next two closures after today.
